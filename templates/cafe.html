Our Story

A corner of the day that’s yours

We started Example Coffee Co. with one rule: make the kind of place we’d want to linger in. Direct-trade beans from growers we know by name, roasted in small batches a few feet from where you sit.

Warm light, good music, and a barista who remembers your order. Stay for one cup or stay for the morning.
