The room

One long room above the hardware shop, six chairs, and a window that faces east.

We opened in 2011 with two chairs and a secondhand backwash. Not much has changed except the number of chairs. Every appointment starts with a conversation about your hair, your routine, and how much time you actually want to spend on it in the morning — and if the honest answer is none, we’ll cut for that.

— Renata, Marcus, Priya & Dee
