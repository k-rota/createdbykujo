01 about me

I'm a recent graduate in Computer Information Systems — Software Engineering, and an unapologetic builder. Give me an empty editor and a problem and I'm happy.

createdbykujo started because I kept noticing the same templated, forgettable websites everywhere. I'd rather bend the rules, mix motion with restraint, and ship something that actually feels like you — while keeping it fast and secure under the hood.

When I'm not shipping, I'm tearing apart how something works just to rebuild it better.
